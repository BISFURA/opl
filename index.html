Who we are

What is Third Space?

A research group at the University of Toronto exploring how technology, society, and everyday life shape one another.

Third Space conducts research in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and Information and Communication Technologies for Development (ICTD). We study technology in context, with particular attention to the experiences and knowledge of marginalized communities.

Our work draws on Critical Theory, Design Fiction, and Political Science, alongside computational approaches such as Mobile Computing, Social Computing, Natural Language Processing, and Augmented Reality.

We welcome students and collaborators from Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Information Science, Design, Anthropology, Sociology, and Gender Studies.
